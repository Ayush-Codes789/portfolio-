paper.md

      

        
Edge-Optimized Lightweight Residual CNN for Real-Time Tomato Irrigation Status Detection

        
IEEE Transactions on Artificial IntelligenceUnder review

        
Dharmendra Dangi, Ayush Jaiswal, Sabita Kumari, Amit Bhagat

        

          
99.98%five-fold cross-validation accuracy

          
20,955images across 4 irrigation levels

          
+0.83 ppover the published baseline

          
50%less training time

        

        
    
          
  • Refined the training framework over a ResNet-50 CNN to classify 4 irrigation levels.
    • 
          
  • Corrected 5 methodological flaws: stratified k-fold CV, label smoothing (epsilon 0.05), boundary-preserving augmentation, extended early stopping, and global seed control. Results were checked with Cohen's Kappa.
    • 
          
  • Applied Kaiming initialization and automatic mixed precision, cutting training time by 50% with consistent gains in precision, recall, F1-score, and kappa.
